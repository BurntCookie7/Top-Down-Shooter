# Audio Wizard

For those who are tired of blindly changing parameters of audio effects in Godot.

> [!NOTE]
> Supported engine versions: 4.6+

![preview](readme_images/preview.png)

**Supported effects:**

- All filters
- All EQs
- Amplify
- Capture
- Chorus
- Compressor
- Delay
- Distortion
- Hard Limiter
- Panner
- Stereo Enhance
- Phaser
- Pitch Shifter
- Reverb

**TODO:**

- Spectrum Analyzer — maybe

**Unsupported effects:**

- Record — nothing to visualize
- Limiter — deprecated in favor of Hard Limiter

**Additional visualizations:**

- AudioStreamPlayer3D attenuation property

    ![AudioStreamPlayer3D](readme_images/AudioStreamPlayer3D.png)
